About Crane Training International

CTI is a Houston-based crane, rigging, and lifting-equipment training center serving the Gulf Coast, with onsite programs across the U.S. and Latin America.

Our Focus

Train to the Standard

CTI was built around a simple idea: crane and rigging training should be measured against the same written standards that govern the job site — OSHA regulation, the ASME B30 series, and the relevant ANSI consensus standards — delivered in the language your crew actually works in.

That means every program, whether taught in English or Spanish, uses the same curriculum outline, the same practical evaluation criteria, and the same documentation.

Mission

Why Bilingual Training Matters

Delivered in English or Spanish — same technical content, same written and practical evaluation. Built for mixed-language crews on the Gulf Coast. On the Gulf Coast, crews are frequently mixed-language — CTI's bilingual delivery means every operator, rigger, and signalperson on a crew is evaluated to the same standard, regardless of which language they trained in.
