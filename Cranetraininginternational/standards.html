Standards & Compliance

CTI curriculum is aligned with, based on, and referenced to the standards below. CTI is an independent training provider; program completion reflects CTI's own evaluation, not a third-party accreditation. How This Works

From Regulation to Curriculum

Each CTI program lists the specific standards it is aligned with on its program page. OSHA regulations (29 CFR) set the legal floor for training and qualification; the ASME B30 series and ANSI consensus standards provide the detailed technical criteria CTI uses to build curriculum, practical evaluations, and inspection checklists.

Where a program prepares operators for employer or third-party certification, that requirement is the responsibility of the employer or the certifying body — CTI documents training and evaluation performed at its facility.
