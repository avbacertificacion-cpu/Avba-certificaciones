Training Programs

Eleven certification programs across operator, rigging & safety, and supervision & inspection tracks. Every program is available in English or Spanish, at our Houston facility or onsite.

MC-100

Mobile Crane Operator

40 hours Operator Certification

Load charts, rigging fundamentals, and safe operating procedure for truck-mounted, rough-terrain, and crawler cranes.

Program Details
TC-120

Tower Crane Operator

40 hours Operator Certification

Setup verification, load geometry, and safe operation of hammerhead, luffing, and self-erecting tower cranes.

Program Details
OH-110

Overhead & Gantry Crane Operator

16 hours Operator Certification

Safe operation of top-running and under-running overhead and gantry cranes in plant and fabrication settings.

Program Details
PIT-140

Forklift / Powered Industrial Truck Operator

8 hours Operator Certification

Classroom and practical training for safe forklift operation in warehouse, yard, and jobsite environments.

Program Details
TH-150

Telehandler Operator

8 hours Operator Certification

Safe operation of telescopic handlers, load charts, and attachment-specific hazards.

Program Details
AW-160

MEWP Operator: Scissor & Boom Lifts

8 hours Operator Certification

Classification, fall protection, and safe operation of scissor lifts and articulating/telescopic boom lifts.

Program Details
RS-130

Rigging & Signalperson

24 hours Rigging & Safety

Rigging hardware, sling angle calculations, and standardized hand and radio signals for crane lifts.

Program Details
FP-200

Fall Protection Competent Person

16 hours Rigging & Safety

Fall hazard recognition, system selection, and jobsite oversight at the Competent Person level.

Program Details
LD-310

Lift Director & Site Supervisor

16 hours Supervision & Inspection

Lift planning, authorization, and jobsite coordination for routine and critical lifts.

Program Details
CI-210

Crane & Equipment Inspector (Competent Person)

40 hours Supervision & Inspection

Frequent and periodic inspection criteria for cranes and rigging equipment, with compliant documentation.

Program Details
TT-300

Train-the-Trainer / Qualified Evaluator

24 hours Supervision & Inspection

Instructional methods and practical evaluation technique for in-house trainers and qualified evaluators.

Program Details

Not a Course

Field inspection service for cranes, rigging accessories, and fall-protection equipment — with a written report and equipment tagging. See the Inspection Services page for scope and process.
