Operator Certification

PIT-140 — Forklift / Powered Industrial Truck Operator

Classroom and practical training for safe forklift operation in warehouse, yard, and jobsite environments.

Objective

Provide the classroom instruction and practical evaluation needed to operate powered industrial trucks safely.

Who Should Attend

New and experienced forklift operators requiring initial certification or a refresher/evaluation.

  • Duration: 8 hours
  • English & Spanish
  • Classroom + Practical

Curriculum

Course Content

  • Truck types, stability triangle, and load capacity
  • Pre-operation inspection checklist
  • Safe travel, stacking, and pedestrian awareness
  • Ramps, docks, and confined-area operation
  • Refueling and recharging safety
  • Practical driving and load-handling evaluation

Evaluation

Written exam plus a site-specific practical evaluation, consistent with employer certification requirements.

Standards Referenced

  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178
  • ANSI/ITSDF B56.1

Training is aligned with and referenced to the standards above; see Standards & Compliance for how CTI applies each one.

Bilingual Delivery

English or Spanish — Same Standard

Delivered in English or Spanish — same technical content, same written and practical evaluation. Built for mixed-language crews on the Gulf Coast.
