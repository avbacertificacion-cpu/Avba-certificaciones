FP-200 — Fall Protection Competent Person

Fall hazard recognition, system selection, and jobsite oversight at the Competent Person level.

Objective

Qualify personnel to identify fall hazards, select appropriate fall protection systems, and oversee fall protection compliance as a Competent Person.

Who Should Attend

Supervisors, safety personnel, and crews working at height who need Competent Person-level fall protection knowledge.

  • Duration: 16 hours
  • English & Spanish
  • Classroom + Practical

Curriculum

Course Content

  • Fall hazard recognition and hierarchy of controls
  • Personal fall arrest system components and inspection
  • Anchor point selection and rescue planning
  • Guardrail, netting, and warning line systems
  • Program documentation and jobsite auditing
  • Practical inspection and hazard-assessment exercise

Evaluation

Written exam plus a practical equipment inspection and hazard assessment exercise.

Standards Referenced

  • OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M
  • ANSI/ASSP Z359

Training is aligned with and referenced to the standards above; see Standards & Compliance for how CTI applies each one.
