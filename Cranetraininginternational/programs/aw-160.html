AW-160 — MEWP Operator: Scissor & Boom Lifts

Classification, fall protection, and safe operation of scissor lifts and articulating/telescopic boom lifts.

Objective

Prepare operators to safely operate scissor lifts and boom-type mobile elevating work platforms (MEWPs).

Who Should Attend

Operators of scissor lifts, articulating boom lifts, and telescopic boom lifts.

  • Duration: 8 hours
  • English & Spanish
  • Classroom + Practical

Curriculum

Course Content

  • MEWP classification (Types 1-3, Groups A/B)
  • Fall protection and guardrail requirements by machine type
  • Pre-use inspection and workplace hazard assessment
  • Stability, wind rating, and ground conditions
  • Rescue planning and emergency descent
  • Practical operating evaluation

Evaluation

Written exam plus practical operating evaluation.

Standards Referenced

  • ANSI/SAIA A92.22
  • ANSI/SAIA A92.24

Training is aligned with and referenced to the standards above; see Standards & Compliance for how CTI applies each one.

Bilingual Delivery

English or Spanish — Same Standard

Delivered in English or Spanish — same technical content, same written and practical evaluation. Built for mixed-language crews on the Gulf Coast.
