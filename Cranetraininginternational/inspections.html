Equipment Inspection & Documentation

A field service, not a course: CTI inspectors evaluate cranes, rigging accessories, and fall-protection equipment on your site and deliver a written report referenced to the applicable OSHA and ASME B30 criteria.

Scope of Service

What We Inspect

  • Mobile, overhead, and tower crane inspection (frequent and periodic)
  • Rigging hardware and sling inspection
  • Below-the-hook lifting device inspection
  • Fall-protection equipment inspection (harnesses, lanyards, anchor hardware)
  • Removal-from-service tagging and documented findings

Deliverables

Report & Tagging

Written Report

Item-by-item findings referenced to the applicable standard, with photos and a pass/fail determination.

Tagging

Equipment is tagged in service or removed from service per the inspection findings.

Recordkeeping

A copy of the report is provided for your maintenance and compliance files.

Reference Standards

Inspected Against

  • OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.179 & 1910.180
  • ASME B30.2, B30.5, B30.9, B30.10, B30.20, B30.26
  • ANSI/ASSP Z359
