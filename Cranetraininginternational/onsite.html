Onsite & Client-Site Training

CTI instructors travel to your facility or job site across the U.S. and Latin America, delivering the same curriculum, evaluation, and bilingual instruction as our Houston training center.

How Onsite Training Works

Four Steps, One Standard

Step 1

Scope the Program

We confirm equipment types, headcount, language mix, and the standards your site requires.

Step 2

Schedule & Logistics

CTI travels to your site with instructors, materials, and evaluation equipment — in the U.S. or Latin America.

Step 3

Deliver Classroom + Practical

Same curriculum and evaluation as our Houston programs, run on your equipment and site conditions.

Step 4

Documentation

Each participant receives a completion record; your site receives a training roster and evaluation summary.

Coverage

Gulf Coast, U.S. & Latin America

Based in Houston, CTI regularly serves the Gulf Coast industrial corridor and travels nationally for onsite programs. For clients with operations in Mexico, Central America, or South America, CTI delivers the same curriculum in Spanish with U.S.-standard evaluation criteria.

  • Training on your equipment, in your operating environment
  • English or Spanish instruction, same evaluation standard
  • Group scheduling for crews and shift rotations
