Train to the Standard.

Crane, Rigging & Lifting Equipment Training for the Gulf Coast

Crane Training International (CTI) is a Houston-based training center for mobile, tower, and overhead crane operators, riggers, signalpersons, and equipment inspectors — with classroom instruction, hands-on yard practice, and onsite programs delivered anywhere in the U.S. and Latin America.

Why CTI

Bilingual Training, Built for Mixed Crews

Delivered in English or Spanish — same technical content, same written and practical evaluation. Built for mixed-language crews on the Gulf Coast.

English / Spanish Instruction

Every program is delivered in English or Spanish with identical technical content and the same written and practical evaluation — no shortcuts for either language track.

Aligned with OSHA, ASME & ANSI

Curriculum and evaluation criteria are built around 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC, the ASME B30 series, and the applicable ANSI consensus standards for each equipment category.

Houston Classroom & Yard

Our training center sits in the Houston Ship Channel industrial corridor, with classroom space and a practical training yard, plus onsite delivery across the Gulf Coast.
