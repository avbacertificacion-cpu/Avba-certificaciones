Houston Training Center

CTI's classroom and practical training yard sit in the Houston Ship Channel industrial corridor, built to serve the Gulf Coast's crane, rigging, and lifting-equipment workforce.

Classroom

Instruction Space

Classroom sessions cover load charts, regulatory requirements, rigging calculations, and inspection criteria, with materials available in English and Spanish.

Training Yard

Practical Evaluation Yard

Practical evaluations run in our training yard on mobile, overhead, and rigging equipment, giving operators and riggers hands-on evaluation time that mirrors real job-site conditions.
